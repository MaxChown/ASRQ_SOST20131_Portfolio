### Why reproducibility Matters?
Reproducibility matters because it ensures that findings are not just random but are based on a systematic and rigorous process. This transparency allows other researchers to verify the results, contributing to the building of trust within the social research community. Furthermore, reproducibility helps detect errors in the researchg process and supports further replication studies.
